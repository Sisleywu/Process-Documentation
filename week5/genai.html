01

Selected Artwork

Summer (Été)

Alphonse Mucha, 1896, Art Nouveau style

Medium: Color lithograph

Alphonse Mucha was born in 1860, he moved to Paris in 1887 and leapt to fame with the overnight success of his commercial poster for actress Sarah Bernhardt. Rather than superficial commercial illustrator, Mucha considered himself an artist first and foremost. He created The Seasons series in 1896, his first design for a decorative ensemble of artistic lithographs. In this series, Mucha drew nymph-like women set within seasonal elements and embodied the mood of each stage in the natural cycle.

Art Nouveau is an important artistic movement during the period which Mucha created The Seasons series. It emphasized flowing lines, organic forms, floral patterns, and the integration of art into everyday life.

Mucha's work reflects these characteristics through the elongated female figures, flowing hair, decorative borders, and stylized plants. This artwork is created in Paris, women in everyday Parisian fashion often wore structured dresses and corsets, but Mucha portrayed them wearing loose, flowing garments inspired by classical and idealized imagery. At that time, Paris was experiencing the expansion of mass advertising, consumer culture, and developing technologies. Artists are increasingly considered for commercial posters, calendars, and decorative prints that could reach a much wider audience than traditional paintings.

I was drawn to the feeling giving by this artwork and the underlying concept. What I find particularly interesting about Summer is how Mucha transforms an abstract concept of seasons into a human figure. In this print, Summer is not simply sunlights, landscapes, or a good weather. Instead, Mucha depicts summer through the woman's body language, her relaxed posture, the flowers in her hair, and capturing the warm atmosphere.

I really liked Mucha's style, especially his organic lines and posture of the figure. I think in Summer, the woman's loose dress, exposed shoulders, long hair, and relaxed posture all created a sense of freedom and intimacy with nature. This kind of lines and shapes gives vibrancy to the artwork, making the woman become alive on the paper rather than just portrayal of figure.

The techniques used in the Summer were also fasinating to me. This piece was produced using color lithography. I experienced several different printmaking techniques before, such as woodblock printing and etching. I think printmaking is a quite magical process and it creates very subtle, decorative images. I think the texture seen from Mucha's Summer are soft and beautiful. Printmaking is also unique because it is purely a human-made process in illustration. Nowadays we use digital arts a lot, but after my experience with printmaking, I started to realize that there is a lot of things that digital art tools cannot mimic, for example the textures of metal panel, spilled paint, lines from brush.

02

Visual Analysis

Composition

Summer uses a vertical, rectangular composition with a woman positioned at the center. Her body was posed in gentle diagonal movement, from her head and shoulders toward her legs and feet. Her soft posture contrasts with the structured rectangular border, creating a balance between organic and geometric forms. The surrounding plants and vines frame the figure, guiding the viewer's attention toward her eyes, making audiences to directly look at her.

Color

Mucha uses soft, muted pastel colors overall with some highlights. There were large amount of pale green, turquoise, cream, peach, and warm yellow. The red flowers in the woman's hair create a stronger color contrast and draw attention to her face. The colors blend harmoniously, creating a warm, peaceful, and dreamy summer atmosphere.

Space & Depth

The woman occupies most of the composition, making her the dominant visual element in the piece. The surrounding plants and landscape are smaller and less detailed, emphasizing the figure. The perspective is relatively shallow. Instead of creating a realistic sense of depth, Mucha utilizes fading colors to create a gentle feeling of space and depth. This gives the artwork a balance between illustration and ornamentation.

"Summer" by Alphonse Mucha, 1896

Medium

The original artwork was created through color lithography, which creates a smooth, printed appearance. The figure and plants are rendered with subtle shading, with delicate outlines.

Rhythm

One of the most outstanding qualities of the artwork is its use of flowing organic lines and repeated curved shapes. Rhythm is suggested through the repetition of flowing, curved lines. The woman's long hair, twisting vines, and folds of fabric follow similar organic patterns that flows through the scene. These curves guide the viewer's eyes through the composition. These natural, irregular lines also implies growth and movement. The artwork mainly uses organic forms. The woman's body, hair, and surrounding plants introduced flowing curves. and composition uses several overlapping shapes, suggesting a visual relationship between the human figure and the environment.

Symbolism

I am particularly interested in how Mucha uses visual elements to personify summer and communicate a seasonal atmosphere. The woman personifies summer, representing the season through feeling rather than through a literal landscape. Her relaxed posture symbolizes warmth, leisure, and stillness. The flowers, vegetation, and soft sunlight represents natural growth and seasonal abundance. Her bare feet touching the water also builds a physical connection with nature.

Initial Prompt for AI Recreations of Summer

Initial Prompt

A vertical Art Nouveau color lithograph with a soft pastel color palette of pale turquoise, sage green, warm cream, muted yellow, and coral pink. A young woman with long, flowing brown hair sits on a rock beside a shallow pool of water, occupying most of the composition. Her body is facing right side, slightly away from the viewer, while her head turns back toward the viewer's right. Her elbows are resting in the air, and her hands touching her chin and lips, creating a relaxed posture. She wears a loose, flowing ivory dress with delicate folds of fabric draping over the rock. A crown of red flowers decorates her head. Her bare feet is in the shallow water and extend toward bottom right of the composition.

There are twisting vines, delicate leaves, and thin branches surround the woman, supporting her hands and around her back. Her long hair, the folds of her dress, and the curling plants create a repeated rhythm of elegant, organic lines, visually connecting the human figure with nature.

The background consists of a pale turquoise/blue sky with soft clouds, and subtle warm yellow sunlight from the bottom right. The lighting is gentle and diffused, creating a warm, quiet, dreamlike summer atmosphere. The composition combines a softly shaded, dimensional human figure with flat decorative botanical elements. Fine outlines, flowing curves, stylized plants. The overall image should feel harmonious, elegant, and quiet, with a sense of stillness and natural beauty.

See my image Prompt, the final image should be a finished color lithograph, there should be no sketch lines, pencil marks, no construction lines.

ChatGPT

This piece is created by ChatGPT, it takes approximately 1 minute to generate. The overall composition and posture is accurate and natural. The flowing sense of plants and organic lines were good. However, the composition lacks negative space, making it feel overly crowded, especially for the dress folds and vines. It is also bit a different from what I expected, it's more like a oil painting of goddess instead the feeling of summer. I think its because the hair is just flowing, which was tangled with the figure in Mucha's art. The humid, lush and warm summer vibe was lost in this piece.

Gemini

Gemini's recreation takes about 10 seconds, it looks like a tarot card to me, with negative space similar to the original, the overall composition and spatial arrangement are quite similar compared to Mucha's piece, especially the balanced use of vines interacting with the figure without overwhelming it. Nonetheless, it loses a lot of details in the color shades and lines. The color scheme is less vibrant, with flatter and more simplified colors, making the result feel more like a graphic illustration. However, Gemini missed the part were I said “her head turns back toward the viewer” and is only looking right.

Midjourney

MidJourney's recreation has a completely different composition and posture from what I described in the prompt. I noticed that both Gemini and MidJourney placed the figure's elbows on her knees, which differs from my instructions. I think this might be because the posture makes more sense in a realistic scope, and there might be more references in their training data. However, MidJourney is the only AI that successfully captures the variation in the weight of outlines, while ChatGPT and Gemini use uniform weight of lines throughout their images. This one also introduces warm reddish sunlight from the left and accidentally connects the vines with the woman'shair. It has a relatively stronger relationship between the figure and nature. Overall, I think MidJourney captures the vibrant summer atmosphere and Mucha's visual style the most. But all three of them lack of the organic feelings and textures from printmaking.

Collage

Collage Materials

My Collage

I combined the AI generation image and a few photos I took that makes me think of the concept of summer. I tried to create a collage that presents my feeling about summer.

Story Prompt

Story Prompt: A girl is holding a glass of juice made using lotus leaves and fountain water from the forest. There is a summer fairy around this cup of drink wearing flowing ivory dress and have a crown on her head with flowers.

AI Generations

ChatGPT

ChatGPT followed the general concept of my prompt, including the girl holding a drink made from lotus leaves and forest fountain water, as well as the summer fairy wearing an ivory dress and a flower crown. I was surprised that it creates a drawing which continues the style I asked for the previous generation of Mucha's artwork. This kind of illustrative style is not what I expected to have, it feels somewhat similar by the composition and color scheme. I think it has a very strong memory of what you did before in relation to what you are going to make after that. There were a lot details that are messed up, such as the six fingers of the fairy or the ambiguous structure of trees behind.

Gemini

Gemini's generation is much more realistic, that the main figure is almost like real person. But I think the fairy is somewhat off because it is so cartoonish that it doesn't fit with the rest of the image. Gemini actually missed the part where I asked the drink to be made up of lotus leaves, this realistic image didn't show the abstract concept of my collage about summer. I was surprised that there isn't any frauds in the image, every makes sense in this generation, while also losing artistic quality.

Midjourney

MidJourney's generation took a more graphic, photographic approach in respond to my prompt. I think it is very interesting that MidJourney incorporates space and depth in the generation, that it made the scene feels like an advertisement of a summer drink. I do like the color scheme and lighting that it gives. However, it ignored the prompt of having the water from a fountain in the forest.

At this point I would not call these generations my own art because they are very different from what I expected and they do not communicate the whole sense of my concept. Because these generative AI can only follow the prompts given by me, and as my prompt is somewhat ambiguous, it will generate images that are not completely based on my idea. There is an interesting comparison in the generations that ChatGPT has a really strong memory, and it tried to create things that suits for your current need. It guessed that I would want a illustration instead of image. In contrast, Gemini takes a more realistic and straightforward approach, which I personally find less creative and somewhat boring. My favorite among these are the generations from MidJourney, it demonstrates a stronger artistic sensibility, producing the most appealing color palette and composition among the three models.

Another interesting observation is the choice of physical appearance. Both MidJourney and Gemini generated girls with tan skin and brown hair, even though I only mentioned girl and fairy in my prompt. I wonder whether the models associate natural imagery with certain physical appearances, or whether these choices reflect patterns and potential biases in their training data. This makes me realize that AI actually could generates images are not entirely neutral, and they reflect visual assumptions that I did not intentionally have.

07

Reflection

Throughout this project, I experimented with ChatGPT, Gemini, and MidJourney to explore how different generative AI tools interpret the same visual descriptions in various approaches. With the detailed prompt for Mucha's Summer, these GenAIs did a relatively successful job in recreating an existing artwork, they seemed to understand straight forwards and descriptive languages well.

One thing I noticed is that AI are quite good at understanding individual objects, materials, colors, and realistic stuff, but struggles with spatial relationships and style. For example, they make mistakes when recreating Much's artwork eventhough it was described clearly. I think this reveals some of the models they read because they can only produce generations based on existing compositions, so they generate a composition that suits the most artworks with similar description with my prompt. When the style and composition are not defined, they interpreted posture and the relationship between the figure and the environment differently.

I think MidJourney creates the most unexpected results, it produces an interesting composition and very different perspective every time. It even cropped out most of the woman's face for my sketch prompt. It also makes the most obvious mistake in all the generations. I think MidJourney would require the most additional prompting if I wanted to achieve my exact vision, because it frequently prioritize visual aesthetics over the specific elements I described. I would need to be much more precise about framing, posture, and small details. In comparison, Gemini's results are the most predictable. It generates the same composition no matter how my story changes when the composition is not strictly defined. It also tends to place the main figure in the center. ChatGPT had a extremely decorated style that it adds many repeated elements and details to the image. But the details of those crowded scenes were off and ambiguous.

Changing my prompts sometimes produced completely different results, especially when I moved from recreating Summer to the collage, sketch and performance. However, there are certain visual tendencies remained consistent within each AI. Even when the subject changed, the models often returned to similar styles and compositions. ChatGPT tended toward detailed, harmonious illustrations, it has the least realistic sense. Gemini often produced photo-realistic and straightforward compositions. MidJourney emphasized artistic, graphic composition, and have photographic features. These differences influenced how I evaluated the image generations. For example, although MidJourney did not accurately recreate my orchid sketch, its unusual cropping created a mysterious style that I found interesting.

Through these experiments, I realized I actually have more control over the concept when creating an artwork by myself. When I made my collage inspired by Mucha's Summer, I could directly decide how the illustrated figure interacted with the photograph and how to express the topic of summer. With AI, I had to translate these decisions into direct language, losing the meaning behind present a feeling or concept. Although AI could generate detailed images much faster, it was difficult to communicate concepts and they failed to understand the emotional atmosphere I wanted.

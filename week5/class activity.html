01

Through the eye of a needle, slow and steady wins the race?

This is what ChatGPT gives us. The idea of needle is presented with just a realistic needle, and the slow and steady part were represented with snails. Kind of cliche.

02

Our Artwork

We choose to do the opposite way with ChatGPT, that we want to show a concept of being fast can also do things perfect. We created a chopping board and knife using cardboard and paper, also made some vegetables on the chopping board. When people are using knife to chop, we can either chop it fast or slow depending on how delicate we want the food to be, and the knife makes this process steady.

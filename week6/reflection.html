Reflection

I choose the objects that came up to my mind immediately. They are things that have a importance to me personally, and they tells story about who am I. I think the process of selection is actually interesting because it helps me to recall many memories that I don't usually think of. Sometimes I asked myself what makes me the person I am today, and through these objects, I realize that things I own and cherish hold a significant part of my identity. They are icons, symbols and representations of my experiences. They reflect the values I hold, and they show the journey of my life.

Some of them I just choosed existing ones, while others I created or modified to better represent my memories and experiences. For example the labels and tages, I pined them together to create a sense of how much stuff I bought usually. When I was choosing and modifying, I figure out how each object contributes to my life, and what things I prioritize. Little Sheep is the first one that pop up when I see this project, it is indeed the most important object in my life. Not only it is a comforting toy I have had since childhood, but it also been through so many moments with me, witnessing my growth and experiences. I found out that a lot objects shows some relationships around me that I value. They represents the moments I spent with my friends, in which shows that I really see friendship as a source of energy for my life.

I think process is reflective, rooting me in my own experiences and helping me understand myself better. From these objects, I see what I don't see usually, I feel the familar emotions once again, and I gain a deeper understanding of my own life and identity.

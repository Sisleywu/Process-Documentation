50 Ways of Seeing

Week 02 — Ideation & Prototyping

Reflection

I decided to do 50 fried eggs because I like them as a breakfast food. I start off by going to arts stores and getting everything that I think is possible to make a fried egg with. I brainstormed 50 different ideas about how I see fried egg in my life, and as an artist. I did drawings of fried egg first, and then experiment on what different styles of fried egg I fit into. It was fun to create those weird fried eggs, one of them that I especially enjoy is words one. I used completely words to make up a graphic fried egg. It is more of a designer perspective in this one that I need to recall what feeling the fried egg brings me and how would I describe it. Which I think is a good way of thinking and reflecting about a single object, and see what does it brings to me. However, not every idea I brainstormed of was feasible, like I thought of using chopsticks to compose a fried egg but I don’t have enough of them, so what ended up was like only an outline.

Since my background is illustration, so 2D visual ideas were pretty easy to come up with. One of them that I like is the one using fried egg to present time, each stage of fried egg would represent a number on the clock. I think this one exhibit my strength of using daily object to communicate, and also prompt me to think about an object in different systems or languages. Through out the process, I did stuck many times that I run out of ideas. A big reason to that is I was always afraid of not creating a successful image. But at the same time, it also forces me to experiment, even though I worried, I just have to continue. For example, the one with egg yolk can’t really be called successful as the yolk is way too transparent for painting. But it still makes it an interesting one to explore because this a material that I wouldn’t usually use. This process really helped me to realize that sometimes the outcome isn’t only thing that I’m looking at, the journey of creating is also essential to understand and think beyond the boundaries. And this is how we, as artists, became creative.

There are many of them that I wish to continue, like the interactive ones and the memory ones. I think they are interesting and there are a lot of rooms to go further, and make it more playable or valuable. I also kind of like them being put all together, that they shows my style of thinking. By the project, I started to understand about how to took minor things into creative projects, and the process of visualizing small ideas to piece a big image.

Week 01 Reflection

Reading Response

In the first reading, Baldwin discussed the idea of art being a kind of solitude. I think this is quite an interesting idea that I usually think art as a way of expression. For me, as an artist, I would use art to communicate with others, and I think it is an important way for me to make connection with the world. However, through this reading, I think it is also true that creation and idea itself is indeed solitude. Because the ideology itself is never replicable, when creating a piece of art, I actually enjoy the process of continues thinking, it feels like digging into a theory that had never been reached. I think what Baldwin mentioned about artist bringing the challenge up front is the actual truth about making art. When I was working with my portfolio, I always like to explore about some hidden phenomenon or fact about this world that had not been talked about. The process of reflection and contemplation is a kind of meditation to me, and it feels like I'm learning and seeing something I don't normally see.

In The Creative Process and The Creative Habit, this journey is been explained clearly. From this reading, I found the idea of creativity is not a rare ability especially interesting and I agreed with idea as it was explained further. For me, I draw from whatever I saw with my eyes, and when I was creating an artwork, I talked about what I observed from the surrounding. Mundane things turns out into big issues, a sentences, a piece of leaf or a thrown tissue were actually pieces of puzzle which creates the image called creativity. I'm always interested in biology, and as mentioned in both of latter two readings, I think it intriguing to say that idea have a natural rhythm. I think it is similar to forming a biological matter by cells that when small things gather, the conclusion will naturally came by as it makes a difference. This indeed could not be called a complete talent, but rather a habit of remembering trivial things around us, it is a way of thinking and a mindset of absorbing everything into your own ideology.
